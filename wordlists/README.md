# Wordlists

Diceware-compatible wordlists shipped with hkdf-tree.

## `eff_large.txt`

The EFF Large Wordlist, published by the Electronic Frontier Foundation and
licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Source: <https://www.eff.org/dice>
- Size: 7 776 words (`6^5`, one word per five-die roll).
- Format: one entry per line, `<roll>\t<word>` (tab-separated).

The word count of 7 776 gives ~12.9 bits of entropy per word.

Attribution: “EFF Large Wordlist” by the Electronic Frontier Foundation.
